## Short Description

A calm typing practice app with ambient soundscapes, personalized daily sets, and no accounts required.

## Full Description

LoKey Typer is a typing practice app built for adults who want quiet, focused sessions without gamification, leaderboards, or distractions.

Choose from multiple practice modes:

- Focus: Calm, curated exercises for building rhythm and accuracy.
- Real-Life: Practice with emails, forms, messages, notes, and other everyday writing.
- Competitive: Timed sprints with personal bests for those who want speed.
- Daily Set: A fresh set of exercises generated each day, adapted to your recent sessions.

Study is its own page, not another practice mode. Add a text file or paste a passage. The app gives it back one piece at a time, in order or shuffled. That text stays on this device and is not saved as a practice run.

The app includes an ambient sound system that creates a non-rhythmic, evolving soundscape designed to support focus without distraction. Sound is always optional and never required.

All data stays on your device. There are no accounts, no cloud sync, and no telemetry. Preferences, run history, and personal bests stay on the device. Text you add for Study stays on the device too.

The installed app works offline.

## Notes for certification

No account and no password. Leave Credentials empty.

Launch LoKey Typer. Home lists Focus, Real-Life, Competitive, Daily, and Study.

Open Focus and type the passage. The gear opens settings. Keyboard sound and ambient sound can be turned off there. The ambient level is a slider.

Open Study, paste a short paragraph, choose In order or Shuffled, and start. Close the app and open it again. The text is still there. A finished piece is not added to practice history.

Screen reader mode and reduced motion are in settings. Screen reader mode turns ambient sound off and shows one short line.
